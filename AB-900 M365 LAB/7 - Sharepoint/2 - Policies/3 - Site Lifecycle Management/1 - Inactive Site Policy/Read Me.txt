It prevents site sprawl (hundreds of abandoned, empty sites), keeps the data storage footprint clean, and protects company data without requiring manual audits.

Policies > Site Lifecycle Management

	Inactive policy
	
		Using this Policy surely help administrator to automate task site management

		Automates Cleanups: Deletes or archives old, forgotten sites without manual IT work.
		Saves Storage Costs: Frees up your limited pooled SharePoint storage capacity.
		Reduces Security Risks: Removes stale sites that still contain active guest sharing links.
		Ensures Data Ownership: Identifies orphaned sites where the original creator has left the company.

		Scope I chose all sites

		Configure policy 

			I set 3 months before the sites set to inactive from the last activity

			Skipped the exclude site owner's or admins to receiving request

			I selected the Site owners and Site admins to certify the sites to keep it active

			Selected "Take an enforcement" and chose enforcement type to "Read-only access" to view only but no edit
			On the finish step we name the policy to "Inactive Sites Policy", added description of the policy and 				Policy mode to "Simulation" for the generate report not notification
